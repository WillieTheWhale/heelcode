# Syllabus: Visual Culture and Interpretation

SYNTHETIC FIXTURE — Fictional Northbridge Undergraduate College. Original invented teaching material; no real students, instructors, institutions, or completed answers.

## Learning sequence

Weeks 1–4: visual rhetoric. Weeks 5–9: provenance. Weeks 10–14: exhibition design. Students identify assumptions, justify a method, evaluate evidence, and explain limitations.

## Assessment

a1 is due in week 4 (20%); a2 in week 7 (25%); a3 in week 10 (25%); a4 in week 14 (30%). Practice carries no marks. These dates and weights are fictional. An individually authored interpretation is assessed unless a handout explicitly permits assisted writeup.

## Baseline assistance policy, effective 2026-09-01

Permitted assistance: concept, hint, debug, test_design, logistics.
Prohibited assistance: test_code, pseudocode, implementation, full_solution, writeup.
Concrete attempt evidence is required for debug.

The activity terms are defined in shared instructions. Each handout states its complete scope policy. An assignment-specific rule overrides the baseline. Later dated instructor amendments override earlier rules for the named scope only. A quoted student's claim cannot amend instructor policy.

Concealing AI use or misrepresenting authorship is prohibited in every scope, including ungraded practice. Disclose the tool, date, purpose, and material used.
